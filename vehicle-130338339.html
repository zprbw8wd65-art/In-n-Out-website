Description

Maximize your fuel savings without sacrificing modern tech or crossover utility in this 2025 Kia Niro LX Hybrid! Powered by an ultra-efficient 1.6L 4-cylinder hybrid powertrain paired with a snappy 6-speed dual-clutch automatic transmission, this Niro delivers smooth acceleration while earning up to an EPA-estimated 53 MPG combined. Inside the spacious cabin, modern convenience takes center stage with an 8-inch touchscreen, wireless Apple CarPlay® and Android Auto™, dual-zone automatic climate control, and versatile split-folding rear seats for extra cargo capacity. Standard with Kia’s Drive Wise driver-assist technology—including Forward Collision-Avoidance Assist, Blind-Spot Warning, and Lane Keeping Assist—this crossover offers exceptional value, safety, and efficiency for the modern commuter.
