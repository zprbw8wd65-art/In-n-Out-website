Description

Elevate your daily drive with sports-car agility and premium comfort in this 2014 Mazda CX-5 Grand Touring AWD! Powered by a responsive 2.5L SKYACTIV-G 4-cylinder engine generating 184 horsepower, this compact crossover pairs driver-centric handling with impressive efficiency and all-weather traction. As the top-of-the-line Grand Touring trim, it comes packed with luxury amenities including full leather-trimmed seating, heated front seats, a power sunroof, dual-zone automatic climate control, and 19-inch aluminum alloy wheels. Modern technology is at your fingertips with a Bose® 9-speaker premium audio system, touchscreen infotainment with backup camera, Bluetooth connectivity, and Blind Spot Monitoring. Fun to drive, spacious, and highly practical—this CX-5 is ready for your next road trip or daily commute. Visit our dealership today for a test drive!
