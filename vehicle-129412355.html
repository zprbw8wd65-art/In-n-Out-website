Description

This clean, Bright White Jeep Wrangler Sport 2-Door combines classic off-road heritage with everyday driveability, featuring iconic round headlights, a black hardtop, matching fenders, and black alloy wheels wrapped in rugged all-terrain tires. Inside, the durable black cloth interior offers versatile seating with easy rear-seat access, a leather-wrapped steering wheel with integrated controls, push-button start, and a central infotainment screen. Equipped with a shift-on-the-fly 4x4 transfer case, fog lights, and side steps, this iconic SUV is built to handle both weekend adventures and daily commutes with confidence.
