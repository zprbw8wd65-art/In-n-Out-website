Description

Experience refined European engineering, turbocharged performance, and all weather confidence in this 2016 Volkswagen Tiguan S 4Motion! Powered by a punchy 2.0L turbocharged 4-cylinder engine generating 200 horsepower, this compact crossover pairs with a smooth 6-speed automatic transmission and VW's intelligent 4Motion AWD system for a responsive, agile drive in any condition. Inside, the driver-centric cabin comes loaded with premium standard features including V-Tex leatherette seating, keyless access with push-button start, a touchscreen infotainment system, a backup camera, Bluetooth connectivity, and heated front seats. Compact enough for easy city parking yet spacious and capable for weekend road trips, this Tiguan delivers a premium driving experience at an exceptional value.
